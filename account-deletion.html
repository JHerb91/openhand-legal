Account Deletion Request

Last updated: June 2026

OpenHand is operated by OpenHand LLC. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the app.
Go to Settings → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time using either of the following methods:

  • In-app (recommended): Open OpenHand → tap your profile icon → Settings → scroll to the Account section → tap Delete Account → confirm deletion. Your account is deleted immediately.
  • By email: Send a deletion request to openhandapp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, age, bio, occupation, location, photos)
  • Your personality assessment results
  • Your giving profile responses
  • Your match history and compatibility scores
  • All messages sent and received
  • Your notification preferences and settings
  • Your account credentials

What May Be Retained

After account deletion, the following limited data may be retained for the periods described:

  • Anonymized match feedback: If you submitted feedback when declining a match, that feedback may be retained in anonymized form to improve our matching algorithm. It cannot be linked back to you after deletion.
  • Transaction records: If you purchased a Pro subscription, transaction records required for legal and accounting purposes are retained for up to 7 years in accordance with applicable law. These records are maintained by our payment processor and do not include your profile content.
  • Legal holds: If your account is subject to an active legal hold or investigation, data may be retained until the matter is resolved.

Subscription Cancellation

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Pro subscription. Before deleting your account, please cancel your subscription through your device's App Store or Google Play subscription settings to avoid future charges.

Free Tier Profile Retention

Free tier users who stop using the app will have their profile automatically removed from the matching pool after 24 hours of inactivity. Full account deletion must still be completed manually using the steps above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at openhandapp@gmail.com.
