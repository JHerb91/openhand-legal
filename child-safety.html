Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 2026

OpenHand is operated by The Arc-Hives LLC DBA OpenHand App. We are committed to the safety of minors and maintaining a platform that is safe, responsible, and compliant with all applicable child safety laws.

Age Requirement

OpenHand is strictly for adults aged 18 and older. Users are required to confirm they are 18 or older during registration. We do not knowingly allow minors to use the platform. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account will be immediately terminated and their data deleted.

Zero Tolerance for CSAM

OpenHand has a zero tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that sexualizes minors. Any such content is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account termination, data preservation, and reporting to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities.

Prevention Measures

We take the following steps to prevent harm to minors:

  • Age verification requirement at registration
  • Live camera verification to confirm user identity during photo exchanges, preventing the use of minor images
  • No public profile browsing — profiles are only visible to matched users
  • In-app reporting mechanism for users to flag safety concerns
  • Account termination for any user found to be a minor or engaging in harmful behavior

Reporting Concerns

If you encounter any content or behavior that raises child safety concerns, please report it immediately through the in-app reporting feature or by contacting us directly at openhandapp@gmail.com.

We will investigate all reports promptly. If we identify CSAM or imminent danger to a child, we will report to NCMEC via the CyberTipline at www.missingkids.org and to relevant law enforcement.

Compliance

OpenHand complies with all applicable child safety laws including but not limited to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and reports to regional and national authorities when required by law.

Contact

For child safety concerns contact us at openhandapp@gmail.com.
